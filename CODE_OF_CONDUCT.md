# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in PacificBoard a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, sexualized language or imagery, publishing private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban participation for behavior they consider inappropriate.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
